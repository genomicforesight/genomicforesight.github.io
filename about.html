About GFI

🎯 Mission

Our mission is to build predictive computational models that connect human genetic variation — both inherited and somatic — to cellular mechanisms of disease, and to translate these insights into actionable therapeutic hypotheses.

🔭 Vision

We envision a future where genomic data is seamlessly translated into preventive strategies, precision diagnostics, and rationally designed therapeutics — accessible to all.

🧠 Approach

GFI integrates three pillars of modern biomedical research:

  • Population Genomics: Identifying variants, genes, and pathways associated with disease.
  • Single-Cell & Functional Biology: Revealing the cellular consequences of genetic perturbation.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Building predictive models that connect genotype to phenotype and therapy.
