🕸️ Graph Neural Networks

NephroBench-GNN

Completed

A Comprehensive Benchmark of Graph Neural Networks for Drug-Induced Nephrotoxicity Prediction. Evaluates GCN, GAT, GIN with scaffold-based splitting, OOD evaluation, and domain shift analysis. Best model: GIN with ROC-AUC 0.876.

PythonPyTorchPyGRDKitGNN

Residual Graph Fusion (RGF)

Completed

A Lightweight Residual-Gated Graph Neural Network for Heterophilic & Noisy Graph Learning. Achieved up to +41.44% improvement over GCN on heterophilic datasets and +36.67% absolute improvement under 30% feature noise.

PythonPyTorchPyGGNNHeterophily

CELLGRAPH

Completed

A GNN-based framework for single-cell transcriptomics using PyTorch Geometric (GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE). Achieved 98.7% transductive and 97.1% inductive accuracy on PBMC 3k, with an inductive protocol preventing data leakage.
