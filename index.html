About

About GFI

Genomic Foresight Institute (GFI) is an independent research institute focused on computational approaches to biological discovery and disease understanding.

GFI explores the intersection of artificial intelligence, genomics, systems biology, neuroscience, and biomedical data science.

Research Vision

To develop computational methods that help transform complex biological data into interpretable knowledge about biological systems and disease.
