About GFI

Building computational perspectives on complex biology.

Genomic Foresight Institute (GFI) is an independent research initiative focused on computational and AI-driven approaches to biological discovery.

The institute brings together computational biology, machine learning, genomics, systems biology, neuroscience, and biomedical data science.

Our goal is to develop computational frameworks that transform complex biological data into interpretable representations, models, and scientific hypotheses.
