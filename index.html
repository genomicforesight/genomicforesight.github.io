About GFI

Building computational perspectives on biology.

Genomic Foresight Institute is an independent research institute focused on computational approaches to biological and biomedical problems.

GFI brings together ideas from computational biology, artificial intelligence, genomics, systems biology, neuroscience, and biomedical data science.

Research Philosophy

Develop computational methods that are reproducible, interpretable, and useful for understanding complex biological systems.
