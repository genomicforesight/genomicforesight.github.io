Research

Six programs.

GFI is organized around six interconnected research programs spanning computational genomics, single-cell systems, biological AI, brain science, computational medicine, and open research software.

Approach

Computational methods, biological questions.

Our research combines methodological development in machine learning and computational science with concrete biological questions across scales — from molecules to systems to clinical data.

01

Method Development

New computational frameworks, foundation models, and representation learning methods for biological data.

02

Biological Insight

Applying computational approaches to real biological questions in genomics, systems biology, and neuroscience.

03

Open Science

Publishing code, models, and datasets openly to accelerate scientific progress.

04

Cross-Program Collaboration

Research leaders collaborate across programs to bridge computational methods and biological domains.
