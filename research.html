Research Areas

Computational approaches to decode the mechanisms of human disease

🧬 Predictive Genomics

Developing models that predict the functional impact of genetic variants on gene expression, chromatin state, and disease risk.

🔬 Single-Cell Modeling

Leveraging single-cell foundation models to understand how genetic perturbations reshape cellular states and trajectories.

🧫 Somatic Mosaicism

Investigating how acquired mutations in somatic cells contribute to aging, clonal hematopoiesis, and neurodegenerative diseases.

🤖 AI in Medicine

Applying deep learning and large language models to discover therapeutic targets and repurpose existing drugs for precision medicine.

🚀 Flagship Project

In Development

OmniCell: A Unified Framework for Variant-to-Therapy Prediction

From Genetic Variant to Therapeutic Target — In One Cell

OmniCell is an integrated computational platform that connects genetic variants to cellular mechanisms and therapeutic targets. It combines four key components:

  • 🧭 Variant Roadmap: A graph database linking millions of variants to regulatory elements and target genes across tissues.
  • 🕸️ Virtual Cell Atlas: Vector representations of human cell types using single-cell foundation models (e.g., scGPT, Geneformer).
  • 🧬 Mosaicism Engine: Modeling how somatic mutations drive clonal competition and tissue dysfunction over time.
  • 💊 Therapeutic Recommender: LLM-powered suggestions for direct targets, pathway targets, and repurposable drugs.
